**31.** No one is fully _____ from the disease until more effective vaccines are invented.

- (A) durable
- (B) immune

- (D) accessible

- (A) tendered
- (B) inclined
- (C) tilted
- (D) sloped

- (B) implement
- (C) reconcile
- (D) satisfy 2701

- (A) disclose
- (B) enrich
- (C) elevate
- (D) devalue

**35.** Unfortunately, many could see that the public health insurance and its new regulations could _____ the poor from getting the medical care they need.

- (A) invade

- (D) indulge

- (A) conducted
- (B) dedicated
- (C) abducted

- (A) mentality
- (B) majority
- (C) mortality
- (D) morality

- (A) hedge
- (B) sanction

- (A) prestigious
- (B) destructive
- (C) spontaneous
- (D) consecutive

- (A) Detached
- (B) Discharged
- (C) Paralyzed
- (D) Withdrawn

- (A) compromises
- (B) commodities
- (C) comprehension
- (D) compassion

- (A) fragile
- (B) flagrant

- (A) fraternity

- (A) burglary

- (B) attributed
- (C) abbreviated
- (D) acclaimed
