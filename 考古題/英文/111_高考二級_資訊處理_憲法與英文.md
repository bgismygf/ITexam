**三、**英譯中：（10分）Most people would admit that our civilization is decadent and our language— so the argument runs—must inevitably share in the general collapse. It follows that any struggle against the abuse of language is a sentimental archaism, like preferring candles to electric light or hansom cabs to aeroplanes.
